## Entry rules
- Use stable IDs such as E-0002, E-0003, etc.
- State claims narrowly and attribute them.
- Record unknown dates as Unknown; do not guess.
- Status values: Unverified, Corroborating, Verified, Disputed, Superseded, Closed.
- Link primary evidence and material corroboration or contradiction.
- Keep analytic judgments in assessment notes; do not present inference as source fact.
