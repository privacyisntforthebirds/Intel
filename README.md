# Public Threat Intelligence Observatory

A public-source, evidence-led observatory for tracking material risks to U.S. national security, critical infrastructure, cybersecurity, and Ohio communities.

**Operating principle:** distinguish verified events from warnings, capabilities, exercises, speculation, and routine preparedness. Do not infer imminence from generic resilience messaging.

## Mission
- Monitor authoritative public sources and preserve primary-source links.
- Build a dated, auditable record of incidents, advisories, policy actions, and material changes.
- Assess relevance, confidence, uncertainty, and potential civilian impact.
- Translate verified developments into proportionate preparedness guidance.

## Scope
1. U.S. national security and strategic threat reporting.
2. Cybersecurity incidents and advisories affecting critical infrastructure.
3. Energy, water, communications, transportation, healthcare, and government services.
4. Ohio and local public-safety, infrastructure, and emergency-management notices.
5. International developments only where a documented connection to U.S. or Ohio risk exists.

## What this project is not
- It is not a classified intelligence product or an assertion of access to non-public information.
- It is not a prediction engine for societal collapse, war, or imminent attacks.
- It does not publish personal data, doxxing, credentials, exploit instructions, or operationally sensitive details.
- It does not treat unverified social posts, anonymous claims, or sensational headlines as established facts.

## Reporting standard
Every substantive assessment should state what happened; when it happened; when it was reported; who says so; what is independently corroborated; what remains unknown; confidence; relevance; and what would change the assessment.

## Status
Automated collection is limited to verified public feeds from CISA, FBI, and Grove City. ODNI, DOE, Ohio EMA, Ohio DPS, PUCO, Franklin County, and Columbus are manual-only; FEMA is pending interface verification. Other initial source candidates remain unreviewed. No threat level is implied by the existence of this project. Assessments are added only when supported by cited evidence.
