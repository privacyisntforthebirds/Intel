# Ohio monitoring plan

## Purpose
Track verified developments that could materially affect Ohio residents or essential services, with emphasis on central Ohio and Grove City. This is a public-source watchlist, not a claim of local threat activity.

## Priority sectors
1. Electric power and natural gas.
2. Drinking water and wastewater.
3. Telecommunications and internet connectivity.
4. Hospitals, emergency medical services, and public health.
5. Transportation, fuel, and logistics.
6. Severe weather, flooding, and other natural hazards.
7. State, county, and municipal emergency notices.

## Official sources
Start with Ohio EMA, Ohio Department of Public Safety, Ohio Homeland Security, PUCO, Ohio Department of Health, Franklin County, Columbus, and Grove City sources listed in ../sources/SOURCE_REGISTRY.md.

## Inclusion criteria
Record a local item when it contains a confirmed incident or service disruption, an official public warning or protective action, a material infrastructure or continuity notice, or a grant/procurement/policy change directly affecting emergency readiness.

Do not elevate routine meeting notices, generic preparedness messaging, or unverified neighborhood posts into threat findings.

## Local relevance fields
Identify the affected jurisdiction, service area, publication and event dates, official response, expected duration if known, and practical resident implications. Avoid publishing sensitive facility layouts, security gaps, or personal information.
