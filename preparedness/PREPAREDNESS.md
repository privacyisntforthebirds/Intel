# Proportionate civilian preparedness

Preparedness is useful for ordinary disruptions and does not imply that a major emergency is imminent. Follow official local instructions during an actual event.

## Practical baseline
- Keep several days of drinking water and shelf-stable food, adjusted for household size and needs.
- Maintain essential medications and a current medication list; ask a pharmacist or clinician about safe backup planning.
- Keep flashlights, spare batteries, a basic first-aid kit, sanitation supplies, and a battery bank.
- Keep some cash in small denominations if feasible.
- Store copies of key documents and emergency contacts securely.
- Plan how household members will communicate and reunite if phone service is disrupted.
- Include pets, mobility needs, and neighbors who may need assistance.
- Know how to receive official alerts and where local authorities publish instructions.

## Avoid
- Panic buying or taking on debt for speculative scenarios.
- Hoarding fuel or storing it unsafely.
- Using generators, grills, or fuel-burning devices indoors or near openings.
- Sharing unverified emergency claims as fact.
- Ignoring evacuation, boil-water, or other official protective instructions.
