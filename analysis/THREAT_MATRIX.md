# Threat matrix

This is an analytic framework, not a statement that any listed threat is currently active.

| Domain | Example concern | Evidence needed before raising concern | Potential civilian impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cyber / critical infrastructure | Disruption of energy, water, communications, healthcare, transport, or government services | Specific authoritative advisory, confirmed incident, technical indicators, or corroborated reporting | Service interruption and safety impacts |
| Physical infrastructure | Sabotage or damage to essential facilities | Confirmed incident, official investigation, credible corroboration | Localized outage, injury risk, supply disruption |
| Energy security | Grid reliability, fuel supply, extreme-weather stress | Operator/regulator notices, reliability assessments, verified outage data | Power or heating/cooling disruption |
| Public health | Disease event or health-system disruption | Public-health agency data and official advisories | Health impacts and service strain |
| Natural hazards | Severe weather, flooding, wildfire, extreme temperatures | NWS, FEMA, state/local emergency notices | Injury, property damage, displacement |
| Geopolitical escalation | Conflict or coercive activity with plausible U.S. effects | Official statements, documented actions, corroborated reporting | Indirect economic, cyber, travel, or security effects |
| Information integrity | Coordinated false claims during an incident | Agency attribution, forensic reporting, repeatable evidence | Confusion and impaired response |

## Assessment fields
Record domain, scenario, geography, affected sector, time horizon, observed facts vs inference, likelihood, impact, rationale, evidence grade, confidence, uncertainties, alternative explanations, indicators, and last-reviewed date.

## Escalation discipline
A routine exercise, general preparedness campaign, isolated social post, or capability statement is not sufficient by itself to elevate an incident assessment. Escalate only when specific, corroborated evidence supports a material change.
